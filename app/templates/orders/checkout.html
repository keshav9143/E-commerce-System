Checkout Architecture

Inventory rows are locked in ascending product_id order (deadlock prevention via consistent lock ordering). The reserved_quantity is incremented atomically within the same transaction — the full stock deduction happens in the downstream INVENTORY_RESERVE fulfillment task. This two-phase approach lets checkout succeed quickly while reserving capacity, and simplifies cancellation.
